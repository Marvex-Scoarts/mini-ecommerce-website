# Remmaxel Boutique | Mini E-Commerce Storefront

A sleek, dark-luxury multi-department online storefront featuring live category filtering, an interactive shopping cart, dynamic quantity management, smart delivery fee calculations, and persistent cart storage.

 **Shop the Live Store Here:** https://marvex-scoarts.github.io/mini-ecommerce-website/

---

## Features

- **Multi-Department Product Catalog:** Browse 16 curated items across **Streetwear**, **Tech Gadgets**, **Beauty & Skincare**, and **Snacks & Drinks**.
- **Instant Category Filtering:** Filter drops in real time using interactive category pills without reloading the page.
- **Dynamic Shopping Cart:** Add products, increment or decrement quantities (`+` / `-`), and remove individual items with instant UI updates.
- **Smart Delivery & Total Math:** Automatically computes subtotals, applies a ₦2,500 delivery fee on standard orders, and unlocks **FREE Delivery** on orders of ₦50,000 and above.
- **Persistent Cart (`localStorage`):** Keeps your shopping bag saved even if you close or refresh the browser tab.
- **Dark Luxury UI:** Responsive CSS Grid storefront with a sticky frosted-glass navbar and slide-in cart panel.

---

## Built With

- **HTML5** – Semantic storefront layout, filter tabs, and cart drawer
- **CSS3** – Dark-mode luxury palette, responsive CSS Grid, sticky positioning, and hover transitions
- **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6)** – Array filtering/reducing, dynamic DOM rendering, cart state management, and `localStorage` persistence

---

## How to Use

1. Open the live store link on your phone or computer.
2. Browse **All Drops** or filter by **Streetwear**, **Tech**, **Beauty**, or **Snacks**.
3. Click **+ Add to Cart** on any item, then use **`+`** and **`-`** inside your cart to adjust quantities.
4. Hit **Checkout Now** to confirm your order and view your receipt summary!

---

## Run Locally on Your Computer

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the `mini-store` folder.
3. Double-click `index.html` to launch the store in any web browser.
